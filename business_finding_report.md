
# Exploratory Analysis with a Finding

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to explore profitability,
discount patterns, product categories, and regional performance.

## Key Business Metrics
- Total Sales: 2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: 286,397.02
- Average Discount: 15.62%

## Category Analysis
- Highest total profit category: Technology
- Profit from this category: 145,454.95
- Lowest total profit category: Furniture
- Profit from this category: 18,451.27

## Discount Analysis
- Discount group with the lowest average profit:
  30–50%
- Average profit for this group:
  -156.28

## Business Recommendation
Review discount strategies for groups with low average profit.
Evaluate profitability alongside sales volume before changing
discount policies.

## Note
These findings describe patterns in the dataset. They do not
establish that discounts alone caused changes in profit.
